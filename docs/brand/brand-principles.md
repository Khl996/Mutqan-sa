# Mutqan Brand Principles

## Purpose
This document defines the foundational brand principles for Mutqan. It is not a campaign brief, not a redesign task, and not a temporary marketing note. It is the reference that guides how Mutqan should speak, present itself, and make visual and verbal decisions across product, marketing, sales, documentation, and customer success.

## Core positioning
Mutqan is not a simple ticketing tool and should never be presented as only a work order system.

Mutqan is a flexible maintenance, facility operations, and asset management platform designed to build the living operational memory of an organization and gradually support better operational decisions.

Arabic positioning:

> متقن منصة تشغيل وصيانة وإدارة أصول مرنة، تبني الذاكرة التشغيلية الحية للمنشأة وتمهد لقرارات تشغيلية أوضح.

English positioning:

> Mutqan is a flexible maintenance, facility operations, and asset management platform that builds the living operational memory of an organization and supports clearer operational decisions.

## Brand essence

### What Mutqan stands for
- Operational clarity.
- Living operational memory.
- Connected maintenance workflows.
- Trustworthy records.
- Better decisions from real operating context.
- Calm, structured, professional execution.

### What Mutqan does not stand for
- Flashy SaaS decoration.
- Empty AI promises.
- Generic facility stock visuals.
- A simple task list.
- A disconnected module catalog.
- A tool that adds complexity to field teams.

## Brand personality

Mutqan should feel like a calm operational advisor.

The brand personality is:
- Confident, not loud.
- Practical, not theoretical.
- Precise, not complicated.
- Mature, not corporate-heavy.
- Close to the field, not detached from operations.
- Ambitious, but honest about what exists today.

## Voice and tone

### Arabic tone
The Arabic language must feel operational, clear, and natural. It should not sound like a literal translation or like inflated marketing copy.

Preferred Arabic tone:
- مباشر.
- مهني.
- هادئ.
- واضح.
- قريب من لغة التشغيل والصيانة.
- غير مبالغ فيه.

Avoid:
- اللغة الدعائية الصاخبة.
- وعود أكبر من المنتج.
- مصطلحات تقنية غير ضرورية.
- تكرار كلمة “عربي” بطريقة تحصر المنتج أو تقلل شموليته.

### English tone
English copy should be professional and enterprise-ready, but not overly abstract. It should speak to operators, facility managers, and decision makers.

Preferred English tone:
- Clear.
- Operational.
- Direct.
- Confident.
- Practical.
- Evidence-based.

Avoid:
- Generic SaaS language.
- Overused words like revolutionary, ultimate, magical, AI-powered when not accurate.
- Long abstract claims without an operational example.

## Messaging principles

### 1. Sell the operating transformation, not the module list
Do not lead with a list of modules only.

Weak message:
> Mutqan includes work orders, assets, preventive maintenance, inventory, and reports.

Better message:
> Mutqan connects requests, assets, locations, teams, and reports so operational work becomes traceable, reviewable, and easier to manage.

### 2. Always connect work to context
Every message should reinforce that value comes from connecting:

Request → Location → Asset → Work Order → Technician → Proof → Asset Record → Report → Decision

### 3. Evidence over claims
Do not claim intelligence, automation, or advanced decision support unless the product supports it clearly.

Approved phrasing:
- “يمهد تدريجيًا لمساعد قرار تشغيلي.”
- “يساعد الإدارة على رؤية ما يحتاج متابعة.”
- “يحول سجلات العمل إلى مؤشرات أوضح.”

Avoid unless implemented:
- “AI decision assistant.”
- “Predictive maintenance.”
- “Digital twin.”
- “BMS/IoT integration.”

### 4. The field must recognize itself
Marketing and product language should reflect real O&M problems:
- Requests lost in messages.
- Assets without maintenance history.
- Follow-up by calls and WhatsApp.
- Reports that do not explain what to do next.
- Delays with unclear causes.
- Managers relying on personal memory.

### 5. Do not over-emphasize “Arabic-first” externally
Mutqan supports Arabic and English and serves teams in the region. Marketing should not make it sound Arabic-only.

Approved idea:
> Designed for operational teams in the region with a professional Arabic and English experience.

## Visual brand principles

### 1. Calm authority before visual excitement
Mutqan should look trusted, structured, and operationally serious.

Visuals should feel:
- Clear.
- Calm.
- Structured.
- Professional.
- Technically mature.

Avoid:
- Loud gradients.
- Random bright colors.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Overly playful illustration.
- Consumer-app energy.

### 2. Paper and ink for marketing, cool surface for product
Marketing may use warmer paper-like surfaces, storytelling sections, and visual metaphors.

The product UI should remain calmer, cleaner, and optimized for daily work.

The two should feel from the same brand world, but they do not have to be visually identical.

### 3. Visuals must explain operations
Every graphic should explain one of the following:
- A connected workflow.
- An asset history.
- A location hierarchy.
- A risk or signal.
- Proof of completed work.
- A report becoming a decision.

Decorative visuals without operational meaning should be avoided.

## Approved brand metaphors

- Operating graph: connected operational flow.
- Timeline: operational memory.
- Asset passport: identity and history of an asset.
- Proof card: documented completion and evidence.
- Pulse: facility health or operational pressure.
- Signal: something that needs attention.
- Layers: facility, location, and asset hierarchy.
- Connected nodes: relationships between work, assets, people, and decisions.

## Brand claims governance

Before publishing any claim, ask:

1. Is it true in the current product?
2. If it is future-facing, is it clearly framed as a direction rather than a current feature?
3. Does it help the customer understand operational value?
4. Can sales or demo show evidence for it?
5. Does it avoid exaggeration?

If the answer is no, rewrite the claim.

## Naming guidance

Preferred product concepts:
- Operating Graph.
- Asset Passport.
- Proof of Work.
- Decision Brief.
- Facility Pulse.
- Operational Memory.
- Location Pressure.
- Work Order Story.

Use carefully and only when supported by product or roadmap clarity.

## Brand do and do not

### Do
- Speak with clarity.
- Show operational examples.
- Connect every feature to a real workflow.
- Use Arabic and English professionally.
- Present Mutqan as serious, calm, and trustworthy.
- Keep marketing ambitious but honest.

### Do not
- Present Mutqan as a generic CMMS.
- Overpromise AI, IoT, BIM, or Digital Twin.
- Use random facility imagery as the main visual language.
- Overload pages with features without a story.
- Use decorative motion or graphics without operational meaning.
- Make each page feel like a separate brand.

## Employee onboarding summary

Any new team member should understand this in one minute:

Mutqan is not just a system for closing work orders. It is a platform for building operational memory. Every design, message, screen, report, and visual asset should help users understand what happened, where it happened, who handled it, what asset was affected, what evidence was captured, and what decision should come next.
