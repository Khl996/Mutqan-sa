Template specs

  • PDF: A4 and Letter, with 20 to 28 mm margins.
  • Social square: 1080 by 1080 px.
  • Social portrait: 1080 by 1350 px.
  • Story frame: 1080 by 1920 px.
  • Presentation frame: 16:9 with 64 px safe area.

Export rules

Logos

Export SVG for source use, PNG for apps, ICO for favicon, and keep a dark-background version.

PDF templates

Export PDF for review and PNG previews for documentation.

Social cards

Export PNG for static cards and MP4 for motion cards.

How to apply tokens in the repo

Current phase

Keep v2 tokens inside this standalone CSS file. Product files should stay unchanged until a separate implementation task approves adoption.

Future adoption path

  • Map `--mutqan-*` values to the existing app theme variables.
  • Update app icons only after final vector exports exist.
  • Apply component styling in small product areas first.
  • Verify RTL and LTR screens after any product adoption.

What not to do

Forbidden terms

  • Sales Kit
  • Marketing Kit
  • 90-Day Pilot
  • Free trial
  • Book a demo
  • Pricing
  • ROI
  • Lead
  • Conversion
  • Customer acquisition
  • Revenue growth

Guardrails

  • Do not present placeholder graphics as final logo artwork.
  • Do not add commercial calls to action inside visual templates.
  • Do not add strategy, business targets, or future capability claims.
  • Do not mix unrelated icon styles.
  • Do not turn the accent color into the full page background.

Acceptance Checklist

  • المرجع بصري وليس تسويقيًا.
  • لا توجد دعوات شراء أو أسعار أو عروض تجريبية داخل القوالب.
  • الشعار موضح كمواصفات، وليس كملف vector نهائي.
  • كل التوكنز موثقة ومربوطة باستخدام عملي.
  • UI Kit يحتوي أمثلة بصرية فعلية.
  • Visual Asset Inventory موجود وواضح.
  • Figma وCanva handoff واضح وقابل للتنفيذ.
  • ملف ZIP يحتوي مجلد docs/brand/v2 كاملًا.
