Motion Rules

RuleDirectionExport Note
DurationKeep most UI motion between 160ms and 320ms. Keep explainers between 1.6s and 3.0s.Use 24 or 30 fps for video exports.
EasingUse ease-out for reveals and ease-in-out for loops.Avoid bounce and elastic movement.
ColorAccent color marks the active path only.Do not flood full frames with turquoise.
AccessibilityMotion must support reduced-motion alternatives.Provide static PNG fallback for key frames.
